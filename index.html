Launch Control

A launch readiness tracker for an AI model launch. Dependencies forecast the real date and expose the critical path. Evidence-backed gates drive a go/no-go call with written reasons, and the weekly status report generates itself and lists what changed since the last one.

  • Dependency-aware forecast that separates planned overlap from real slips
  • Gates spanning safety, capacity, load testing and billing reconciliation
  • 14 engine tests and 19 browser checks at desktop and mobile widths

APPROACH

How I work, shown rather than claimed.

Make the decision rule explicit

Launch Control writes its go/no-go reasons down, so a team can argue with the rule instead of with each other.

Measure against a baseline

Feedback Lens froze the original retriever before changing it and reports the regressions alongside the gains.

Design the failure states

Loading, empty, abstention and error states are part of the product. No question should end in a dead end.

State the limits

Synthetic data is labelled as synthetic, and anything untested is described as untested.
